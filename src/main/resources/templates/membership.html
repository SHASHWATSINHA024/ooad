How to Join

Follow these simple steps to become a member of our library:

  1. Create an account on our website and select your membership type
  2. Submit your membership request
  3. A librarian will review and approve your membership
  4. Visit the library with a valid ID proof to collect your membership card
  5. Pay the membership fee
  6. Start borrowing books!
